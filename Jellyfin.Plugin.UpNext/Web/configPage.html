Up Next

When a movie reaches its credits, the video shrinks to the corner and the next film counts down, like Netflix. Movies in a collection play in release order; otherwise a similar unwatched movie is picked. Credits are found through the Intro Skipper plugin's credits segments.

Defaults for everyone

Each user can change this for themselves under Settings → Up Next.
Used for movies Intro Skipper hasn't analyzed yet.

Apps

Browsers, Jellyfin Media Player, the Android and iOS apps, and LG webOS TVs. Reload the app after changing this.
Roku, Android TV, Fire TV and other apps that can't show the Up Next screen. The server starts the next movie after the delay below, when the app accepts remote commands.
